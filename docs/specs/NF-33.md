## 1. Executive Summary

Looking down at a glowing screen during high-speed nocturnal pursuits introduces physical trip hazards and compromises visual stealth. Providing automated tactical voice cues from the AI Game Master allows operatives to pocket their phones and play entirely through audio.

**NF-33 (Spoken TTS Narration)** provides:
1. **Standardized Voice Prompt Templates**:
   - `head_start_begin`: "Head start active. Runners, disperse into the zone."
   - `hunters_released`: "Hunters released. Radar sweep online. Stay alert."
   - `hunter_proximity`: "Warning: Hunter closing in. Distance under twenty meters."
   - `zone_shrinking`: "Attention operatives: Tactical zone is contracting."
   - `extraction_ready`: "Extraction portal is now online. Runners, proceed to extract."
   - `player_tagged`: "Target neutralized."
2. **Priority-Driven Tactical Voice Queue**:
   - Urgent events (`priority: 'high'`) preempt standard announcements.
   - Max queue capacity prevents audio lag and stale queue build-up.
3. **Cross-Platform Delivery**:
   - Web `SpeechSynthesisUtterance` and native fallbacks.
