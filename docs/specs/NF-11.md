## 1. Executive Summary

Player progression and milestone recognition drive long-term engagement and group retention. Achievements must reuse existing match telemetry safely without leaking privacy.

**NF-11 (Badges & Personal Records)** provides:
1. **7 Canonical Tactical Badges**:
   - `first_deployment`: Complete first live field mission.
   - `veteran_operative`: Deploy in 10 or more completed missions.
   - `closest_call`: Evade a hunter within 15 meters without being caught.
   - `ghost_whisperer`: Complete a match as an un-tagged victorious runner.
   - `silent_step`: Finish match with noise levels strictly under 60 dB.
   - `clean_sweep`: Win as hunter with 100% catch rate.
   - `marathoner`: Accumulate over 5,000 meters in tactical field movement.
2. **Server-Computed Integrity**:
   - Stored in `player_badges(user_id, badge_id, game_id, metadata, earned_at)`.
   - Evaluated server-side via `award_match_badges(game_id, user_id)` RPC and client evaluator fallback.
3. **Dossier Display**:
   - Displayed in `app/(tabs)/you.tsx` in a horizontal tactical scroll with unlocked vs locked status indicator, unlocked counter chip, and category icons.
