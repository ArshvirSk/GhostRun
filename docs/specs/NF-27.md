## 1. Executive Summary

After an intense mission, players want to review and share their stealth maneuvers, close escapes, or coordinated hunter flanks. Exporting route animations must strictly protect players' physical safety and home locations.

**NF-27 (Replay Trail Animation Export)** provides:
1. **Consent C3 Gating**:
   - Route export is strictly blocked if the player has disabled `share_trail` (Consent C3).
2. **150m Privacy Start/Finish Trimming**:
   - Strips the first and last 150 meters from the path to prevent pinpointing home or workplace origins.
   - Enforces a minimum distance guard (> 350m) to reject excessively short tracks.
3. **Zero Real-World Coordinate Leakage**:
   - Converts raw latitude/longitude points into normalized relative viewport coordinates `[0, width] x [0, height]`.
   - SVG and keyframe payloads contain zero GPS coordinates.
4. **Animated Keyframe Engine**:
   - Yields timestamped frames with trailing history and animated SVG polyline markup for smooth replay rendering.
