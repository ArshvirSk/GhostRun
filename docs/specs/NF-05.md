# GhostRun — Specification: Silent-by-Default Alerts & Tactical Notification Channels (NF-05)

> **Status:** Complete & Verified (Wave 1 · NF-05 · Vibration-Only Channels, Stealth Suppression & Presentation Handlers Passed)  
> **Companions:** `docs/WAVES.md`, `docs/NEW_FEATURES.md §4 (NF-05)`, `src/motion/haptics.ts`, `app/settings.tsx`

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In stealth outdoor evasion games like GhostRun, an audible phone notification chime while hiding behind cover immediately compromises the runner's physical location. 

**NF-05 (Silent-by-Default Alerts)** guarantees that:
1. All Android notification channels for in-game events are configured as **vibration-only** with `sound: null` and dedicated tactile vibration patterns.
2. In-app notification presentation handlers suppress system notification audio while preserving tactile haptics.
3. Users can configure sound behavior via a "Tactical Silent Alerts" toggle in Settings (defaulting to **ON**).
4. Critical channels (`tactical-alerts`, `match-events`, `lobby-comms`) are explicitly initialized on app launch and match start.

---

## 2. Notification Channels Architecture

| Channel ID | Name | Importance | Sound | Vibration Pattern | Use Cases |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tactical-alerts` | Tactical Radar & Ping Alerts | `HIGH` | `null` (None) | `[0, 150, 100, 150]` | Delayed pings, proximity pulses, heat sensed, boundary warnings |
| `match-events` | Match Lifecycle & Phase Shifts | `HIGH` | `null` (None) | `[0, 300, 150, 300]` | Countdown start, live phase, role assignment, victory/defeat |
| `lobby-comms` | Lobby Invites & Comms | `DEFAULT` | `null` (Silent default) | `[0, 100, 100, 100]` | Lobby invites, ready check prompts |

## 3. Implementation Details

1. **`src/lib/notifications.ts`**:
   - `initTacticalNotificationChannels(soundEnabled?: boolean)`: Configures the 3 Android notification channels with null sound by default.
   - `setupNotificationPresentationHandler(soundEnabled?: boolean)`: Registers `Notifications.setNotificationHandler` with `{ shouldShowAlert: true, shouldPlaySound: soundEnabled, shouldSetBadge: false }`.
2. **`app/settings.tsx`**:
   - Stored preference `@ghostrun:tactical_silent_alerts` (default `'true'`).
   - Switch in Tactical Settings to toggle audio vs tactile-only.
3. **App Initialization**:
   - Invoked on startup in `app/_layout.tsx` and before match deployment in `src/game-shell/GameShell.tsx`.
