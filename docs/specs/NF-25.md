## 1. Executive Summary

Outdoor field conditions—including harsh sunlight, dynamic movement, and visual impairments—require rigorous accessibility tooling to ensure all players can compete fairly and safely.

**NF-25 (Accessibility Extras)** provides:
1. **Calibrated Colorblind Palettes**:
   - `Protanopia` (Red-weak): Bright tactical blue (`#60A5FA`) vs. amber gold (`#FBBF24`).
   - `Deuteranopia` (Green-weak): Sky cyan (`#38BDF8`) vs. tangerine orange (`#FB923C`).
   - `Tritanopia` (Blue-weak): Mint green (`#4ADE80`) vs. rose crimson (`#F43F5E`).
2. **HUD Scaling Multipliers**:
   - `standard` (1.0x), `large` (1.25x), and `extra_large` (1.5x) for outdoor readability while sprinting.
3. **Screen Reader Tactical Cues**:
   - `formatA11yHudAnnouncement` formats directional clock coordinates, proximity alerts, and boundary warnings into clear voice narration for TalkBack and VoiceOver users.
4. **Local Persistence**:
   - Settings persist locally using `@ghostrun:a11y_colorblind` and `@ghostrun:a11y_hud_scale`.
