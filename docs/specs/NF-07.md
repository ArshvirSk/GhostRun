## 1. Executive Summary

Tactical team coordination is critical during live matches. Players need silent, rapid communication without compromising stealth through voice calls.

**NF-07 (Team Radio) & NF-08 (Report, Block & Moderation Basics)** provides:
1. **Team-Isolated Comms Channels**:
   - Runners channel and Hunters channel are strictly separated by RLS policies.
   - Operators can access their team channel.
   - Tagged players can remain in read/write comms to relay intelligence.
2. **Tactical Quick-Chips**:
   - One-tap operational dispatch chips:
     - `"Hunter nearby!"` (danger)
     - `"I'm safe"` (safe)
     - `"On the move"` (neutral)
     - `"Need help"` (warning)
     - `"Regroup"` (neutral)
     - `"Clear area"` (safe)
3. **Safety & Moderation Safeguards**:
   - 200-character transmission length ceiling.
   - Rate limiting: max 1 message/sec burst, max 20 messages/minute.
   - Built-in profanity masking.
   - Report action (`reports` table) with structured reasons (`harassment`, `spam`, `hate_speech`, `other`).
   - Block action (`blocks` table) with immediate client-side and server RLS suppression.

## 2. Server Architecture

### 2.1 Database Schema
- `blocks(user_id, blocked_user_id, created_at)`
- `reports(id, reporter_user_id, reported_user_id, game_id, message_id, reason, details, status, created_at)`
- `team_messages(id, game_id, team, sender_player_id, sender_user_id, sender_name, kind, body, created_at)`

### 2.2 RPCs
- `send_team_message(p_game_id, p_kind, p_body)`: `SECURITY DEFINER` validating membership, rate limits, length, and inserting row.
- `block_user(p_target_user_id)`: Registers a user block.
- `unblock_user(p_target_user_id)`: Removes a user block.
- `report_player(p_reported_user_id, p_game_id, p_message_id, p_reason, p_details)`: Logs moderation report.
