## 1. Executive Summary

Looking at phone screens during tactical sprints introduces visual distraction, trip hazards, and compromises night stealth. A smartwatch companion app allows players to receive wrist haptic alerts and perform essential safety check-ins and emergency SOS triggers with a single tap.

**NF-24 (Wear OS Protocol)** provides:
1. **Bluetooth Wearable Messaging Contract**:
   - `HUD_UPDATE`: Streams remaining round duration, nearest opponent distance, and directional clock hour (1–12).
   - `HAPTIC_PULSE`: Fires silent vibration cadences for radar sweeps, hunter proximity warnings, and tag confirms.
   - `CHECKIN_REQUEST`: Displays wrist prompt for night curfew.
2. **One-Tap Wrist Actions**:
   - `CHECKIN_CONFIRM`: Clears curfew alert immediately.
   - `TRIGGER_SOS`: Dispatches emergency distress beacon from the wrist.
   - `QUICK_RADIO`: Sends canonical team radio chips (e.g. `hunter_nearby`, `im_safe`).
3. **Battery & Screen Optimization**:
   - Monochrome high-contrast OLED mode minimizing watch battery drain during long operations.
