## 1. Executive Summary

In-game local haptics and silent alerts keep active players concealed. However, alerting players when the app is backgrounded or closed (for incoming lobby invites, match countdowns, and scheduled games) requires remote push notifications.

**NF-12 (Remote Push Notifications)** provides:
1. **Push Token Lifecycle**:
   - Device tokens (Expo/FCM) registered in `user_push_tokens(user_id, token, platform, categories, is_active, updated_at)`.
   - RPCs `register_push_token` and `delete_push_token`.
   - Auto-deletion upon user account purge or sign-out.
2. **Channel-Aware Payload Dispatcher**:
   - `lobby_invites`: dispatches to `lobby-comms` with high priority.
   - `game_starting`: dispatches to `tactical-alerts` with high priority.
   - `scheduled_reminders`: dispatches to `match-events`.
3. **Category Preferences**:
   - Granular user preferences allowing players to mute specific notification types.
