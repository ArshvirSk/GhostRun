## 1. Executive Summary

Squads and gaming communities frequently organize operations over Discord. Providing webhooks allows organizers to automate match announcements and post-match tactical debriefs directly into server channels.

**NF-22 (Discord Integration)** provides:
1. **Webhook URL Validation**:
   - `validateDiscordWebhookUrl` ensures endpoints match legitimate Discord webhook domains (`discord.com/api/webhooks/...`).
2. **Tactical Rich Embeds**:
   - Match Creation: Dispatches operational alerts with room code, mode name, host handle, and one-tap join links.
   - Post-Match Debrief: Posts victory summaries, duration, distance, and top speed.
3. **Strict Location Privacy**:
   - Zero raw latitude/longitude coordinates or private route traces are exported to Discord.
