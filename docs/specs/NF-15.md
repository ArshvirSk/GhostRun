## 1. Executive Summary

End-of-season and monthly recap dossiers ("GhostRun Wrapped") provide players with an engaging, shareable synthesis of their tactical performance across patrols. It boosts organic viral retention and squad bragging rights.

**NF-15 (Season Recap / "Wrapped")** provides:
1. **Aggregated Tactical Metrics**:
   - Total operations / deployments.
   - Overall victory rate and role-specific wins (Runner vs. Hunter).
   - Total patrol distance (km) and top sprint speed (km/h).
   - Longest evasion survival time.
   - Primary tactical doctrine (most played game mode).
   - Commended badges unlocked.
   - Assigned operational rank tier (`Recruit`, `Operative`, `Specialist`, `Apex Predator`).
2. **Strict Privacy Guarantees**:
   - Zero GPS coordinates, route traces, pinpoint addresses, or match timestamps are ever exported.
   - Complies with Consent C3 and NF-10 privacy rules.
3. **OS Share Sheet Export**:
   - One-tap formatted briefing text exportable to messaging apps, social channels, or squad discords.
4. **UI Integration**:
   - Dedicated "Season Recap" modal integrated into the Case File / Profile tab (`app/(tabs)/you.tsx`).
