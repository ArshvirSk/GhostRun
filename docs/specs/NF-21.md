## 1. Executive Summary

Configuring iOS support ensures GhostRun can be deployed and distributed via Apple TestFlight and the App Store with rigorous background location and privacy compliance.

**NF-21 (iOS Configuration & Universal Links)** provides:
1. **Apple Bundle Configuration (`app.json`)**:
   - `bundleIdentifier`: `com.ghostrun.app`
   - `buildNumber`: `12`
   - `supportsTablet`: `false` (optimized for handheld mobile sprinting)
2. **Privacy Permissions & Info.plist Justifications**:
   - `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription`: Game boundaries and radar pings during live tactical matches.
   - `NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription`: Background location alerts when phone is pocketed.
   - `NSMotionUsageDescription`: Motion activity sensor checks to detect foot sprinting vs. vehicle transport.
   - `NSCameraUsageDescription`: Tactical QR code scanning for squad and lobby invites.
   - `UIBackgroundModes`: `["location", "fetch", "remote-notification"]`
3. **Apple Universal Links & Associated Domains**:
   - `applinks:ghostrun.app` and `webcredentials:ghostrun.app` configured in `app.json`.
   - `public/.well-known/apple-app-site-association` JSON payload declaring `applinks` routing for `/join` and `/join/*`.
