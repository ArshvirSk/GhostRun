## 1. Executive Summary

Scaling GhostRun from neighborhood squads (4–12 players) to municipal tournament events (50–150 players) requires an architecture that avoids per-row database change serialization bottlenecks and prevents live screen-peeking.

**NF-23 (Event Mode)** provides:
1. **Realtime Broadcast Channels**:
   - Replaces database RLS polling with lightweight, stateless Supabase Broadcast topics.
   - Dedicated team topics (`event:<id>:runners`, `event:<id>:hunters`, `event:<id>:referees`).
2. **Delayed Spectator Stream**:
   - Sliding window buffer enforces a minimum 30–60 second delay before streaming telemetry to spectator feeds, neutralizing ghosting/screen-peeking.
3. **Referee Controls & Safeguards**:
   - Designated referee role to pause, resume, and confirm live field tags.
   - Capacity gating: 10 to 150 players maximum.
   - Requires Broadcast Consent C6 per game.
