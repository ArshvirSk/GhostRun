## 1. Executive Summary

A seasonal framework and remote announcement banners establish a shared operational rhythm and provide players with clear countdown targets (e.g. "Season ends in 24 days") and updates on community events.

**NF-31 (Season Framework & Promo Banners)** provides:
1. **Seasonal Timing Framework**:
   - `seasons(id, number, name, starts_at, ends_at, is_active)`
   - Computes dynamic human-readable countdowns ("Season ends in X days / hours").
2. **Remote Announcement Banners**:
   - `announcement_banners(id, title, subtitle, link_url, starts_at, ends_at, is_active, created_at)`
   - Remote-config-driven promotion slot on the Play tab for community announcements or mode spotlights.
3. **Optimized RPC**:
   - `get_active_season_and_banners()` fetches the current season and active banner list in a single query.
4. **Client UI & Fallbacks**:
   - `PlayScreen` banner bar rendering active season countdown and announcements with offline resiliency.
