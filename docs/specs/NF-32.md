## 1. Executive Summary

App Store ratings and responsive support establish credibility and player confidence. 

**NF-32 (In-App Review Prompt & Contact)** provides:
1. **Google Play Store Rating**:
   - Direct link to store listing: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ghostrun.app`.
   - Accessible via Settings $\to$ "Rate GhostRun on Play Store".
2. **Tactical Support Mailto**:
   - Prefilled support intent via `mailto:support@ghostrun.app`.
   - Includes app release version (2.7.4), device OS, and platform timestamp.
   - Strictly excludes coordinates or player passwords.
3. **Safety Timing**:
   - Store rating action is placed in Settings for user-initiated engagement rather than interrupting high-stress active gameplay moments.
