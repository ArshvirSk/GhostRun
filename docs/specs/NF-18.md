## 1. Executive Summary

Players encountering bugs or issues in the field need a frictionless, in-app channel to submit intelligence without opening third-party platforms.

**NF-18 (In-App Feedback & Diagnostics)** provides:
1. **In-App Report Modal**:
   - Accessible via Settings $\to$ "Transmit Field Feedback".
   - Categories: `bug`, `suggestion`, `safety`, `other`.
   - Transmission body up to 2000 characters.
2. **Safe Diagnostics Attachment**:
   - Optional hardware & environment bundle: app version (2.7.4), OS platform, OS version, timestamps, and permission states.
   - **Zero GPS coordinates** and zero player names attached.
3. **Server Persistence**:
   - Stored in `feedback(id, user_id, category, message, bundle, created_at)`.
   - RPC `submit_feedback(p_category, p_message, p_bundle)`.
