## 1. Executive Summary

Operational perimeter boundaries define the playing arena. When players breach the boundary, self-enforcing rules maintain game integrity without human referee intervention.

**NF-09 (Boundary Rule Options)** introduces 4 operational boundary enforcement options:
1. **`warn`**: Advisory UI alert and audio/haptic pulse only. No telemetry or penalty broadcast to hunters.
2. **`extra_ping`**: Immediate single ping broadcast to hunters when the boundary grace period expires.
3. **`reveal_live`**: Frequent pings (every 5 seconds) released to the hunter squad until the runner re-enters the operational zone.
4. **`auto_tag`**: Runners remaining outside the zone for 120 seconds are automatically eliminated (`status = 'tagged'`) with event reason `auto_tag_boundary`.

## 2. Technical Architecture

### 2.1 GPS Drift & Accuracy Gating
- **Drift Tolerance Buffer**: 5-meter inner buffer (`DRIFT_BUFFER_METERS = 5`) to prevent false triggers along perimeter lines.
- **Accuracy Gating**: Fixes with accuracy $\ge 35\text{m}$ (`MAX_ACCURACY_THRESHOLD_M = 35`) are gated with caution margins to eliminate GPS noise false positives.
