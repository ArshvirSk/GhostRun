## 1. Executive Summary

As games expand in duration, area, and nocturnal play, safety is paramount. 
`NF-06 (Safety Check-in & Curfew)` provides host-configurable automated safety guards:
1. **Curfew Enforcement**: Round automatically concludes when a designated curfew timestamp is reached, returning all players to safety.
2. **Periodic Safety Check-in**: In night or extended matches, active players receive a discrete 60-second prompt: *"Are you OK?"*.
3. **Missed Check-in Alert**: If a player does not acknowledge the prompt within 60 seconds, a `checkin_missed` event is broadcast strictly to their teammates with their last known coordinate to check on them, without leaking their position to opponents.
