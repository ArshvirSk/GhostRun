## 1. Executive Summary

Supporting international squads expands GhostRun's reach and allows players globally to experience in-game tactical alerts, role briefings, and safety curfews in their primary language.

**NF-20 (Localisation Engine)** provides:
1. **Multi-Language Support (5 Locales)**:
   - English (`en`, default)
   - Spanish (`es`)
   - German (`de`)
   - French (`fr`)
   - Japanese (`ja`)
2. **Standardized Translation Dictionary**:
   - Covers common actions (`common.*`), tactical roles (`roles.*`), game modes (`modes.*`), real-time in-game alerts (`alerts.*`), and safety/SOS prompts (`safety.*`).
3. **Type-Safe API**:
   - `t(key, params?, overrideLocale?)` ensures compile-time key validation and prevents runtime missing string failures with fallback to English.
   - Dynamic locale switcher `setLocale(locale)` and getter `getLocale()`.
