## 1. Executive Summary

Scheduling matches ahead of time coordinates squads, increases lobby attendance, and makes GhostRun sessions recurring community rituals.

**NF-13 (Scheduled Games & Calendar Reminders)** provides:
1. **Scheduled Match Database & RLS**:
   - `scheduled_games(id, host_id, title, mode_id, scheduled_at, area_name, notes, created_at)`
   - `scheduled_game_rsvps(scheduled_game_id, user_id, response, updated_at)`
   - RPCs: `create_scheduled_game` and `rsvp_scheduled_game`.
2. **RFC 5545 iCalendar Generator**:
   - Generates standard `.ics` payloads with `BEGIN:VCALENDAR`, `VEVENT`, `UID`, `DTSTART`, `DTEND`, `SUMMARY`, `LOCATION`, `STATUS:CONFIRMED`.
   - Lines exceeding 75 characters are folded per RFC 5545 standard.
   - Text values are escaped against commas, semicolons, backslashes, and line breaks.
3. **Strict Privacy Gating**:
   - Exact GPS coordinates and residential addresses are disallowed in scheduling fields.
   - Only high-level rendezvous zones (e.g. "Sunset Park North") are permitted until lobby live launch.
4. **Host & Squad UI**:
   - `ScheduleGameModal` with tactical timing presets, mode chips, and one-tap calendar export/sharing.
