## 1. Executive Summary

Persistent squads ("Crews") allow players who frequently patrol together to stay connected across sessions, share join codes easily, and coordinate future deployments.

**NF-16 (Persistent Crews & Squad Lists)** provides:
1. **Minimal, Privacy-Safe Schema**:
   - `crews(id, name, code, created_by, created_at)`
   - `crew_members(crew_id, user_id, role, joined_at)`
   - Strict privacy: No live coordinates, locations, or presence data are ever associated with crews.
2. **Squad Roles & Lifecycle**:
   - Roles: `captain` (founder/leader) and `member`.
   - RPC `create_crew(name, code)` automatically assigns creator as captain.
   - RPC `join_crew(code)` allows squad mates to join with a 4-8 character code.
   - RPC `leave_crew(crew_id)` reassigns captaincy if captain departs or disbands empty crew.
3. **Data Protection & Deletion**:
   - `ON DELETE CASCADE` ensures profile deletion purges all crew memberships cleanly.
4. **Squad UI**:
   - `CrewManagementModal` supporting my squads roster, code sharing, squad founding, and code joining.
