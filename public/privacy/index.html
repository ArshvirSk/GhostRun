Privacy Policy & Data Guarantees

Last updated: November 2026. Your privacy and physical security are our highest priorities.

1. Ephemeral Location Telemetry

GPS coordinates are streamed to the server exclusively during active rounds. Exact locations are discarded once rounds conclude. We do not maintain historical logs of your continuous routes.

2. 150m Start & Finish Privacy Trimming

Whenever you choose to export or share a round summary card, the first 150 meters (your origin) and the last 150 meters (your destination) are trimmed from any route data to protect home or work locations.

3. Granular User Consents (C1–C7)

GhostRun strictly enforces user choices for noise analysis (C2), route sharing (C3), leaderboard participation (C5), and crash telemetry (C7). You can revoke any consent at any time in Account Settings.

4. Zero Third-Party Advertising

GhostRun contains zero ad networks, data brokers, or marketing trackers. Your movement data is never sold or shared.

5. Right to Erasure

You can permanently delete your account and all associated records directly in the app via the "Purge All My Data" button in Account Settings, or by emailing privacy@ghostrun.app.
