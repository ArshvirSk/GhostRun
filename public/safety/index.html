Operational Safety Protocol

GhostRun is an intense outdoor pursuit game. Safety is non-negotiable.

1. Road & Traffic Awareness

Never run into traffic, cross against pedestrian signals, or trespass on private property. The game will never incentivize crossing dangerous terrain or motorways.

2. 18+ or Guardian Supervised

GhostRun is strictly rated for adults aged 18 and older, or minors participating under active, direct guardian supervision.

3. Night Curfews & Check-ins

When playing after sunset, hosts can enable mandatory safety check-ins and curfews. Players must tap their check-in button every 10–15 minutes.

4. Emergency SOS

An emergency SOS button is always visible on your game screen. Activating SOS instantly reveals your status to all players and provides direct links to local emergency dispatch.
