In-App Bug & Telemetry Dispatch

For live connection or GPS sensor diagnosis, open the app, head to Settings → Send Feedback & Bug Report. This allows you to attach an anonymous device diagnostics bundle with zero GPS coordinates.

Account Deletion & Data Privacy

To erase your profile and records, visit Settings → Purge All My Data within the application or send an email to privacy@ghostrun.app.
